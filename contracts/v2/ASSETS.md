# FIN11 assets and explicit depreciation — contract v1

Technical convention `anniversary-cumulative-half-up-v1`, under approved manual Q02 allocation and Q10 IDR20,2. No production values or automatic season split. `GET/POST assets`, `GET/PATCH assets/{id}`, `POST assets/{id}/cancel`, `GET assets/{id}/audit`; same paths for `depreciation-allocations`. Existing operation envelope, strict fields, manager+assigned GH, current authorization before replay, global identity and sync-state lock, version/reason/audit/receipt/feed transaction. Paginated list filters GH/state and allocations additionally asset/season. All financial feed data is manager-only.

Asset requires an active `asset_acquisition` expense in the same GH. Active asset costs sharing a payment sum to at most that payment; payment correction/cancel cannot undercut live reservations. Operating allocations still reject asset payments. Positive integer `life_years`1–1000 is a representational limit, not an agronomic default; cost A and residual S are nonnegative with S<=A. End of life must fit Gregorian year9999. Name, in-service date and usage basis are explicit. An asset with allocation history cannot change cost/residual/life/date; accepted snapshots do not change on rename. Cancellation requires all active depreciation allocations cancelled first; no history is deleted.

Straight-line exact rational annual D=(A−S)/n. Year k is the half-open interval [original in-service anniversary(k−1), original anniversary(k)), dates in Asia/Jakarta. Leap-day anniversaries clamp to February28 in nonleap years, anchored on the original date. Integer cents C=(A−S)*100; cumulative budget B(k)=HALF_UP(C*k/n) in integer cents, annual budget=(B(k)−B(k−1))/100. This telescopes exactly to A−S, avoiding repeated rounded yearly D exceeding the base. Zero base is legitimate but permits no positive allocations. No daily proration or inferred allocation.

Each manual allocation supplies asset, open season in same GH, usage_year, [period_start,period_end) contained in that year, globally formatted UUID usage_reference, positive money amount, nonblank basis. Reference is a user-selected usage identity, not an actor identity. Asset+reference and asset+identical period are reserved permanently (including cancelled history); correct the original or review its cancellation rather than double-charge that identity. Source/season/year/period/reference are immutable on edit; amount/basis may be corrected with reason/version. Active annual sums<=annual budget and active cumulative sums<=A−S, excluding own previous amount on correction. Overlapping but nonidentical periods with different references remain explicit manual allocations, still constrained by budgets; no time-share interpretation is inferred.

JSON schemas: [asset](asset-operation.schema.json), [allocation](depreciation-allocation-operation.schema.json). SQL schema11 is additive; historical migrations1–10 and frozen schema8 map remain untouched. Native entry/outbox acceptance and independent MySQL concurrency evidence must be recorded separately before FIN11 is complete.
